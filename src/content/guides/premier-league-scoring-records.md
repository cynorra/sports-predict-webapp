Premier League scoring records tell the story of the league through its strikers: Alan Shearer's career total, Harry Kane's goals for a single club, Erling Haaland's debut season and some very short stopwatch entries. This guide collects the scoring records from the Premier League's own records table, which runs up to the 2025/26 season, and explains what each one measures. For the single-season record in detail, see our guide to the [most goals in a Premier League season](/guides/most-goals-in-a-premier-league-season).

Every figure below comes from that league table unless another source is named at the end.

## Career scoring records

Shearer is the league's top scorer with 260 goals for Blackburn Rovers and Newcastle United between 1992/93 and 2005/06. The records page also splits the total in several ways, and the other holders are a useful reminder of how many kinds of scorer the league has produced.

Shearer appears four times in the table, which is why his name sits at the top of so many lists. One detail is easy to miss: Kane's 213 for one club is a separate record from the career total, because Shearer's goals were shared between two clubs.

## Fastest goals and hat-tricks

The league also records time. Shane Long scored after 7.69 seconds for Watford against Southampton on 24 April 2019, the fastest goal in the competition. The fastest hat-trick belongs to Sadio Mané, who needed 2 minutes and 56 seconds for Southampton against Aston Villa on 16 May 2015.

The first Premier League hat-trick was scored by Eric Cantona for Leeds United against Tottenham Hotspur on 25 August 1992. Sergio Agüero scored the most, 12 for Manchester City between 2011/12 and 2020/21, and Shearer scored five in the 1995/96 season, which is the most in a single campaign.

## Big hauls in a single match

Five goals in a match is the league record, and five players have done it:

- Andrew Cole, Manchester United against Ipswich Town, 4 March 1995
- Alan Shearer, Newcastle United against Sheffield Wednesday, 19 September 1999
- Jermain Defoe, Tottenham Hotspur against Wigan Athletic, 22 November 2009
- Dimitar Berbatov, Manchester United against Blackburn Rovers, 27 November 2010
- Sergio Agüero, Manchester City against Newcastle United, 3 October 2015

Defoe's five came in one half, which is a record in itself. Justin Kluivert holds the record for penalties in one match with three for Wolverhampton Wanderers against AFC Bournemouth on 30 November 2024.

## Streaks and calendar records

Consistency produces its own records. Jamie Vardy scored in 11 consecutive matches for Leicester City between 29 August and 28 November 2015. Sadio Mané scored in nine straight matches against Crystal Palace. Kane scored 39 goals in the 2017 calendar year, and Ryan Giggs scored in 21 different Premier League seasons for Manchester United, the most of anyone. Craig Bellamy scored for seven clubs.

The away-goal record for a season is shared by three players on 16: Kevin Phillips for Sunderland in 1999/00, Kane for Tottenham in 2022/23 and Salah for Liverpool in 2024/25. At home, Shearer scored 25 for Blackburn Rovers in 1994/95 in 21 matches and Haaland 22 for Manchester City in 2023/24 in 19 matches.

## The other side: own goals

An own goal is a goal that a player puts into their own team's net, and it counts for the opponents. The records page lists Richard Dunne as the player with the most, 10, scored for Manchester City, Aston Villa and Queens Park Rangers between 2004/05 and 2014/15. It is a useful figure to keep in mind when you look at match results: not every goal in a scoreline was scored by a striker, and models that predict scorelines treat them as part of the total.

## Awards, assists and goal involvements

The league's table lists Andy Cole for Newcastle United in 1993/94 and Alan Shearer for Blackburn Rovers, each with 34 goals and 13 assists, for 47 goal involvements in a 42-match season. Salah matched that total in 2024/25 with 29 goals and 18 assists in 38 matches. Kane and Son Heung-min combined for 47 goals as a partnership for Tottenham, with one assisting the other, between 2016/17 and 2022/23.

On awards, Thierry Henry and Salah each won the Golden Boot four times: Henry in 2001/02, 2003/04, 2004/05 and 2005/06, and Salah in 2017/18, 2018/19, 2021/22 and 2024/25. Giggs holds the assists record with 162 and Bruno Fernandes the single-season mark with 21 in 2025/26.

## Milestone goals

The first Premier League goal was scored by Brian Deane for Sheffield United against Manchester United on 15 August 1992. The 10,000th was Les Ferdinand's for Tottenham against Fulham in December 2001, and the 35,000th was Haaland's for Manchester City against Brighton on 7 January 2026. The youngest scorer is Max Dowman, who scored for Arsenal against Everton at 16 years and 73 days on 14 March 2026, while Teddy Sheringham is the oldest at 40 years and 268 days.

## Using records without being misled

Records are the extremes of a distribution, so they are poor guides to the next match. A typical team scores one or two goals, and the likeliest scorelines are narrow, as the guide to the [Poisson distribution](/guides/poisson-distribution-soccer-goals-explained) shows. Treat a record as context, and use the strength gap, home advantage and expected goals, covered in the guide to [expected goals](/guides/what-is-xg-expected-goals-explained), when you make a pick.
