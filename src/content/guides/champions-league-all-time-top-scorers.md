The Champions League top scorers table has just three players above 100 goals. Cristiano Ronaldo leads with 140 goals on Opta's count, Lionel Messi is second with 129 and Robert Lewandowski third with 109. This guide sets out the top 10, the records behind them, who reached 50 goals fastest and why two respected tables disagree by a goal here and there.

The totals come from the Opta Analyst's ranking, last updated on 4 November 2026, and from UEFA's own all-time statistics. They count goals in the Champions League itself; Opta states that its figures exclude qualifiers.

## The all-time top 10

On Opta's count the leading ten are Ronaldo 140, Messi 129, Lewandowski 109, Karim Benzema 90, Kylian Mbappé 74, Raúl 71, Erling Haaland 62, Harry Kane 58, Thomas Müller 57 and Ruud van Nistelrooy 56. Thierry Henry and Mohamed Salah follow on 50 each.

The list is also fluid: Mbappé, Haaland, Kane and Salah are all still active and have each passed 50 goals, so the order below the top three can change within a season.

## Why two tables disagree

UEFA publishes its own all-time ranking, and it does not match Opta's exactly. UEFA lists Ronaldo on 141 goals from 187 matches, where Opta has 140 from 183. UEFA's top 10 also includes Van Nistelrooy on 60 and Andriy Shevchenko on 59, while Opta lists them on 56 and 48 and has Thomas Müller in its top 10. Opta states that its totals exclude Champions League qualifiers. UEFA's figures are higher than Opta's for some players, which fits UEFA counting matches that Opta leaves out, though neither page sets out the full rule.

For a headline you can say Ronaldo has "about 140" goals. For a precise figure, name the source: 140 per Opta, 141 per UEFA. The order of the top three is the same in both.

## Cristiano Ronaldo: the record holder

Ronaldo's 140 goals came for Manchester United (21), Real Madrid (105) and Juventus (14). The Opta Analyst records several marks that remain his:

- 17 goals in the 2013-14 season for Real Madrid, the most in a single Champions League campaign, followed by 16 two years later
- Seven Champions League top-scorer awards, a record across the European Cup and Champions League eras
- Goals in a record 93 different matches, 51% of his appearances
- Eight hat-tricks, a total he shares with Messi
- Ten goals against Juventus, the most any player has scored against one opponent

He now plays for Al Nassr in Saudi Arabia, so Opta regards it as unlikely that he adds to the total.

## Messi and Lewandowski

Messi's 129 include 120 for Barcelona, the most any player has scored for one club in the competition, and nine for Paris Saint-Germain. He has scored against 40 different opponents, a record until Lewandowski reached 41, and he once scored five in a single match, in Barcelona's 7-1 win over Bayer Leverkusen in March 2012, something Ronaldo never did.

Lewandowski reached 100 Champions League goals against Brest for Barcelona and now has 109, of which 69 came for Bayern Munich and 23 for Barcelona. Opta notes he is only the second player after Ronaldo to score ten or more for three different clubs, and that his two goals against Newcastle United in the 2025-26 round of 16 made him the player who has scored against the most different opponents, 41. He also scored 15 for Bayern in 2019-20, the season they won the trophy.

## Fastest to 50 goals

The Champions League era rewards finishers who arrive early. According to Opta the quickest to 50 goals are:

- Erling Haaland, 49 games
- Ruud van Nistelrooy, 62 games
- Lionel Messi and Harry Kane, 66 games each
- Robert Lewandowski, 77 games
- Kylian Mbappé, 79 games

Haaland's 50th came in September 2025, and Opta adds that his 52 goals in his first 50 appearances is also a record, beating Van Nistelrooy's 43. Mbappé was 25 years and 356 days old when he reached 50, the second youngest after Messi.

## Who could climb the list next

Four active players have a realistic chance of moving up. Mbappé is on 74, and his 15 goals in the 2025-26 season are the most in one campaign by anyone except Ronaldo. Haaland is on 62 and passed Sergio Agüero's 36 for Manchester City, reaching 38 for the club on the second matchday of the 2026-27 season. Kane is on 58, having reached 50 in 66 appearances in March 2026. Salah reached 50 the same month and became the first African player to do so, according to Opta.

Whether any of them reaches Ronaldo's 140 depends on how long they stay at clubs that qualify every year, which is why the table changes slowly even when its stars are prolific.
