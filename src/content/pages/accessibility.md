We want everyone to be able to read our articles and play the prediction game. Our target is conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

## What we do

- Pages use semantic HTML, a single H1 and a logical heading order, and each article has a table of contents.
- The page language is declared as English.
- Prediction inputs have text labels that screen readers announce.
- Interactive elements are standard HTML links, buttons and inputs, so they can be reached with a keyboard.

## Known limitations

We have not yet completed a full manual audit with assistive technologies or a colour-contrast check across every page. The score-entry cards on the prediction page are the area most likely to have gaps, and we are reviewing them.

## Feedback

If you find a barrier, tell us through the [contact page](/contact) with the page address and what went wrong. We will review every report and fix confirmed problems.
