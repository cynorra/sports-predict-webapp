Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them in three ways.

## Essential

These keep you signed in and remember your predictions. The site does not work properly without them.

## Analytics

These help us understand which pages are useful so we can improve the site.

## Advertising

Google and its partners set cookies to show and measure ads and to limit how often you see the same ad. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may limit features such as saved predictions. To control personalised ads, visit [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). See also our [privacy policy](/privacy).
