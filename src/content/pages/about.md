Soccer Scores Today is an independent football and sports website with two jobs: explain matches with data, and let fans test their own judgement against a statistical model.

## Who we are

Soccer Scores Today was founded and is maintained by software engineer and editor [Eren Şimşir](/author). The site combines mathematical forecasting models with plain explanations, and it carries no betting promotions or tips.

## What we publish

- **Match analysis and predictions** for selected fixtures, with a model-based scoreline and win probabilities.
- **Guides** on competition formats, rules and how to read football statistics.
- **A prediction game** where you enter scores before kickoff, earn points and compare yourself with other fans on the leaderboard.

## How we work

Every prediction on the site comes from the model described on our [methodology page](/methodology), and once enough predictions have been made we will publish how it performs, misses included. Our sourcing and correction rules are set out in the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy).
