SkorRadar is a free game and information site. You cannot stake money here and you cannot win money here.

## Our position on betting

We do not promote or link to bookmakers or gambling operators. Any odds shown are "fair odds" calculated from our own model, as explained in our [methodology](/methodology). Treat them as a way to compare the model's view with your own, not as a betting tip. No prediction is certain.

## If you do gamble elsewhere

- Never bet money you cannot afford to lose.
- Set limits on time and spending before you start.
- Do not chase losses.
- Gambling is for adults only.

## Getting help

If gambling is causing problems for you or someone close to you, contact a local support service. In the United States, the National Problem Gambling Helpline is 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537) and offers free, confidential support at all times.
