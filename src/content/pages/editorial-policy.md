## Who writes Soccer Games Today

Every guide and match analysis on Soccer Games Today is published under the name of its author, [Eren Şimşir](/author), who is responsible for what appears on the site. If something is wrong, it is the author's job to fix it.

## Where the facts come from

Rules come from the Laws of the Game published by IFAB and from official competition regulations. Results and fixtures come from football-data.org. Where a figure or a claim needs support, the article ends with a Sources section that links to it. When a rule differs between competitions, the article says so and points to the official document.

## Predictions and opinion

The scores and probabilities on the site come from the model described on the [methodology page](/methodology). They are estimates, not promises, and they are labelled that way. Opinion is kept apart from fact, and nothing on the site is betting advice.

## Automatic match previews

Most match previews in the analysis section are written automatically. Every number in them comes from the model described on the [methodology page](/methodology) and the fixture list from football-data.org. A language model only writes the wording around those numbers, and software checks that no other figures or names appear. The previews do not use team news, injuries or form, and they say so. They go live without a manual read; the author reviews them afterwards and corrects mistakes as described below. Guides are written and read by the author before publication.

## Originality

Each article should explain something clearly or add a calculation, figure or example of its own. Text is not copied from other sites, and the same wording is not reused across articles.

## Corrections

If we get something wrong, we correct it and note the change at the top of the article with the date. To report an error, use the [contact page](/contact).

## Independence and advertising

Advertising does not influence what is written. Soccer Games Today does not accept payment for predictions and does not link to betting operators.
